
.. note::

   **Everything on this page runs on your machine for free.** Local Docker
   environments, BACON manifests, deploying your own checkouts, registering your
   own builds, locks, and stacks or CLI plugins from a public registry namespace
   need no account, tenant or token. Commands that talk to a hosted NeuronSphere
   -- the cloud Artifact Librarian, published-version queries, cloud BOM
   inspection or import -- connect to your organisation's NeuronSphere cloud
   tenant and are marked as such where they appear. See :doc:`/licensing`.
