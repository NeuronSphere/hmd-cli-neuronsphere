.. note::

   **Paid NeuronSphere required for cloud features.** Cloud features described
   in these docs, including cloud Artifact Librarian access, published-version
   queries, and cloud BOM inspection or import, require paid NeuronSphere and
   access to your organisation's tenant. Installing the free ``nsctl`` CLI does
   not provide that access. Local Docker environments, BACON authoring,
   deployments from your own checkouts, and stacks or CLI plugins pulled from
   a public registry namespace do not require a paid cloud tenant.
   See :doc:`/licensing` for product availability and component licences.
